AI Evaluator Assessment Rubric

A structured scoring framework for evaluating how consistently AI response reviewers apply guidelines, justify decisions, and deliver useful feedback.

At a glanceFive observable competencies, each assessed on a four-point scale from Inadequate to Exemplary.

Purpose

Make evaluator quality observable.

This framework supports the selection and training of evaluators working on large language model response assessment. It measures evaluator quality through explicit behaviors rather than impressions alone.

Possible uses include candidate screening, evaluator training and calibration, and portfolio or self-assessment documentation.

Scoring scale

Four levels of performance

Each criterion uses the same scale. The level descriptions distinguish complete, evidenced performance from partial or missing behavior.

4

Exemplary

Complete, with concrete examples.

3

Advanced

Mostly complete; one key element may be missing.

2

Beginner

Partial; important elements are missing.

1

Inadequate

Vague, absent, or off-topic response.

Criteria overview

Five competencies, one consistent lens

Each criterion focuses on evidence that can be identified in the evaluator’s reasoning or written response.

01 / REFLECTION

Bias identification & management

Names at least two biases, explains how they could affect judgment, and identifies a countermeasure.

02 / GUIDELINES

Guideline adherence

Cites the relevant criterion and justifies the score using the instructions rather than personal opinion.

03 / COMMUNICATION

Written feedback quality

Uses clear language, references specific text with timestamps, identifies the issue, and suggests a solution.

04 / CONSISTENCY

Score calibration

Scores similar responses consistently and explains differences with specific references.

05 / METACOGNITION

Reflective judgment

Confirms guideline use, recognizes possible personal influence, and identifies the relevant bias.
